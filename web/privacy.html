Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Your intent never leaves your device. IGNOSIS has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. If you buy rites, the payment provider handles your payment details, and each rite publishes a salted hash of your intent that only you can link back to it.

Who is responsible

Michael Roland, Florida, USA, is responsible for IGNOSIS (ignosis.net and the IGNOSIS apps). Contact: info@ignosis.net.

What stays on your device

  • Your intent. It is turned into a sigil on your device and is never sent to us or stored, even locally; it is cleared when you release the seal.
  • Settings: the skin you chose.
  • If you buy rites: the payment reference for your package, how many rites are left, and for each rite its burn record, commitment and salt (the random value that links the commitment to your intent). These are kept in your browser's storage (or the app's storage on Android) so you can spend your rites and prove a burn is yours. Clearing the site's data deletes them.

We use no cookies. The storage above is not readable by us or by other sites.

What we receive

  • For each rite: the commitment (a SHA-256 hash of your intent combined with the secret salt, which we cannot reverse) and the payment reference it is paid from. We keep these, with the resulting burn record, in our fulfilment ledger so each rite is burnt exactly once and refunds can be handled correctly.
  • Payments on the web (Stripe): Stripe collects your card details, email address and billing details under its own privacy policy. We never see your full card number. Stripe shares with us the payment record: the amount, time and status, and the email address (and name, if asked for) that you entered, which we use for receipts, refunds, disputes and our accounts.
  • Payments on Android (Google Play): Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We receive the order number and purchase token to confirm the purchase and process refunds.
  • Technical data: like any website, our servers see your IP address when you connect. We use it in memory to limit abuse (a cap on requests per minute) and do not store it with your rites. Our hosting providers keep short-lived server logs, which may include IP addresses.

What becomes public

Each rite is recorded on the Solana blockchain, which anyone can read and no one can change or delete: the commitment, the 1,618 Joules burnt, the time, and our treasury's signature. Nothing in it names you or reveals your intent. Without the salt on your device, a commitment cannot be linked to you or to the intent. Our site and API also show recent burns, using the same public data.

Who processes data for us

  • Stripe (web payments) and Google Play (Android payments).
  • Netlify hosts the website; Fly.io hosts our API and ledger in the United States.
  • Helius relays our transactions to Solana; it sees only what is published on-chain.
  • Google serves the web app's rendering engine and fallback fonts from www.gstatic.com and fonts.gstatic.com, so your browser connects to Google when the site loads.

We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and we do not use it to profile you.

How long we keep it

Ledger records and payment records are kept while IGNOSIS operates and for as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally up to seven years). On-chain records are permanent. Hosting logs are kept by our providers for a short period.

Your choices and rights

You can use the studio without giving us any personal data. You may ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to info@ignosis.net; we will answer within 30 days. We cannot delete on-chain records, and we may keep payment records the law requires. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or certain US states), you may have further rights, including to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

IGNOSIS is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Buying rites requires being 18 or having a parent's permission.

Links

The Compendium links to other websites. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and its date, and tell you in the app before a significant change applies.
