Refund Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Unused rites can be refunded for 14 days after you buy them. A rite already burnt cannot be refunded, because its burn is permanent.

What can be refunded

  • Unused rites, within 14 days. We refund the unused share of the package price. For example, if you bought 3 rites and used 1, we refund two thirds of what you paid.
  • Burnt rites cannot be refunded. A burn is recorded on the Solana blockchain the moment you release the seal and cannot be undone.
  • If something went wrong (you were charged but no rite arrived, a burn failed, or you were charged twice), we refund it at any time, not just within 14 days.
  • If your browser's site data was cleared and your unused rites were lost with it, we refund them within the 14 days.

How to ask

Web purchases: email info@ignosis.net from the address on your Stripe receipt, or include the receipt. We reply within 3 business days. Refunds go back to the card you paid with and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.

Android purchases: you can ask Google Play for a refund within 48 hours of buying (play.google.com, Order history), or email us within 14 days as above.

What happens to a refunded package

Once a package is refunded, any rites left in it can no longer be used. Burns already made stay on the blockchain. The same applies if a payment is disputed with your bank.

Your rights

This policy adds to, and does not limit, any rights you have under consumer law where you live.
