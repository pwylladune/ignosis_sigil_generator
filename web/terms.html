3. Rites

A rite is an optional, paid service. When you release a seal with a rite, we burn 1,618 IGNOSIS Joules on the Solana blockchain and record a salted hash of your intent (a "commitment") with the burn. The intent itself is never sent to us or published. Only you hold the random value (the "salt") that links the commitment to your intent, and it stays on your device.

  • Joules are not money. IGNOSIS Joules are a record-keeping token that we create and hold. They cannot be transferred, sold, held by you or anyone else, or exchanged for anything. A rite is a service, not a purchase of cryptocurrency, and it is not an investment.
  • A burn is permanent. Once confirmed, a burn and its commitment stay on the public blockchain and cannot be undone or removed by anyone, including us.
  • Timing. We pay the network fees. A burn usually confirms within seconds. If the Solana network or our service is unavailable, your rite is kept and completed when it is available again.
